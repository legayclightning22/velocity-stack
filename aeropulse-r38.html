Acoustic Inertia Tuning

Every time the CBR 250R intake valve slams shut at 9,000 RPM, the incoming column of fast-moving air has nowhere to go. It crashes against the valve face, generating a high-pressure acoustic rebound shockwave that travels backward through the throttle body toward the airbox.

In a stock rubber intake, this shockwave disperses chaotically into the airbox as turbulent noise.

The AeroPulse R-38 uses a calibrated 50 mm runner depth and elliptical bellmouth. When the acoustic wave strikes the bellmouth lip, it reflects back as a positive pressure pulse timed to arrive back at the intake valve right as it opens again. This creates passive acoustic supercharging without a compressor.

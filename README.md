## 🏁 Executive Overview & The Mission

Factory OEM motorcycle airboxes are designed around noise emissions regulations (Euro 5+), rapid injection-molding budgets, and generic compromises. They feature blunt, flexible rubber funnels that cause severe boundary-layer separation above 8,500 RPM. Worse, modern 270° crossplane parallel-twins suffer from internal **cylinder robbing**, where one cylinder depresses the intake plenum right as the adjacent cylinder opens its intake valve.

**XCD Industries** designs, models, and additive-manufactures bespoke, motorsport-grade intake velocity stacks from continuous-fiber reinforced **ASA Carbon Fiber (ASA-CF)**. By utilizing mathematical acoustic runner staggering and 180° full toroidal bellmouth flares, we transform destructive intake vacuum waves into dense, high-velocity ram filling.

### The Import Disruption
* **Foreign Imports:** Cost upwards of **₹15,000 to ₹25,000** after customs tariffs and import duties, with 60–90 days port transit and zero fitment guarantees.
* **Local Lathe Knock-offs:** Untuned generic aluminum pipes lacking internal tapers, inducing worse flow detachment than stock rubber boots.
* **The XCD Solution:** 100% CFD-modeled, dyno-benchmarked, and Dragy satellite-verified induction systems manufactured domestically in Bengaluru and **dispatched across India in 48 hours** at a fraction of foreign import pricing.

## 🔬 Platform Deep-Dives

### 1. Aprilia RS 457 // AeroPulse TwinSync-36™
The 270° crank pin layout produces uneven 0° – 270° – 720° firing strokes. 
* **The Starvation Flaw:** Cylinder 1's intake valve closes at ~230°. Cylinder 2's intake valve opens just 90° later (~320°). At 10,000 RPM, this occurs in just 1.5 ms. Identical OEM symmetrical stacks cause Cylinder 2 to inhale into an acoustic vacuum void left by Cylinder 1, dropping Cylinder 2's volumetric efficiency by **7.4%**.
* **The TwinSync Fix:** We increase Cylinder 2's runner length to **52.5 mm** (+10.5 mm acoustic delta) while maintaining Cylinder 1 at **42.0 mm**. This phase-shifts the reflected Helmholtz acoustic wave by 90°, delivering positive compression charge right at Cylinder 2's intake valve face:

$$f_s = \frac{c}{2\pi} \sqrt{\frac{A}{V \cdot L_{eff}}}$$

* **Cylinder Balance:** Equalized from 7.4% factory void to **99.4% balance**.
* **Airbox Packaging:** Maintains **21.5 mm** free ceiling clearance to the upper lid (>0.6D boundary safety rule) inside the 74.0 mm vertical plenum cavity.

## 🛠️ Airbox Installation & Clearance Verification

1. **Plenum Access:** Remove tank covers and unfasten 8x Torx T20 airbox perimeter bolts.
2. **Extraction:** Disconnect the OEM symmetrical rubber boots from the aluminum spigots.
3. **Seating:** 
   * Install **Stack A (42.0 mm)** on Cylinder 1 (Left).
   * Install **Stack B (52.5 mm)** on Cylinder 2 (Right).
   * Press down firmly until the **1.2 mm external retention barb** seats with an airtight snap into the spigot rubber boot.
4. **Clay Clearance Test:** Place modeling clay on the bellmouth crown of Stack B. Fasten airbox lid to torque spec. Remove lid and verify clay height measures >= 21.5 mm.

AeroPulse R-38

Dedicated Velocity Tuner for the Honda CBR 250R Platform

Factory air intake funnels prioritize noise reduction and broad production tolerances over boundary-layer aerodynamics. The OEM rubber throat creates turbulent separation rings at the intake mouth, starving top-end volumetric efficiency.

The XCD AeroPulse R-38 incorporates a continuous 7° conical throat taper terminating in a 180° elliptical bellmouth. This geometry utilizes incoming pressure pulses to ram high-velocity air into the combustion chamber right as the intake valve closes, turning atmospheric pressure into passive boost.
